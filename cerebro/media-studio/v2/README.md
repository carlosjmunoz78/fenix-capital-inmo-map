## Clone / identity capability target
The studio is explicitly structured for:
- persistent synthetic characters;
- authorized real-person face/body reconstruction;
- authorized digital doubles;
- authorized voice cloning and voice conversion;
- authorized singing voice;
- video -> persistent character reconstruction;
- motion/performance transfer;
- scene/camera/lighting reconstruction;
- multilingual ADR/dubbing and lip sync.

Real-person execution remains fail-closed unless the rights record contains valid authorization, provenance, revocation and scope. Internet discovery never creates reuse rights automatically.

## Effects / assets target
Effects are first-class assets, not hard-coded presets. Families include particles, fire/smoke, fluids, destruction, electricity/energy, atmosphere/weather, optical/lens/light, fantasy, sci-fi, impact, transitions, motion graphics, Foley, SFX, ambience, music stingers, creatures, crowds and custom effects. Effects may have generated/procedural/captured/licensed/internal origins, versions, parameters, rights and provenance.
