## Why this is the quality floor

The reel is materially stronger than prior CEREBRO canaries because it behaves as a social reel rather than as a technical animation demo:

1. **Immediate hook/problem:** `Estás pagando por NO DECIDIR...`
2. **Narrative progression:** the idea is developed as three consequences / "impuestos" instead of disconnected cards.
3. **Human-first imagery:** people and recognisable situations carry the message.
4. **Scene-purpose alignment:** each person/location supports a specific narrative beat rather than appearing as arbitrary casting.
5. **Readable mobile hierarchy:** large headline blocks dominate each beat.
6. **Variation:** faces, environments and visual treatment change enough to reset attention.
7. **CTA resolution:** `Decidir = paz` → `COMENTA: FAMILIA` → Fénix Capital end card.
8. **Brand closure:** the piece ends with a clear company identity instead of stopping after an effect demo.

## What Productora must preserve from this reference

- hook understandable in ~1 second;
- one clear thesis per reel;
- human/emotional visual anchor;
- causal narrative, not random montage;
- scene changes tied to meaning;
- large mobile-readable text;
- explicit CTA or useful resolution;
- recognisable Fénix brand closure;
- pace suitable for social retention.

## What Productora must improve beyond this reference

This reference is **not** considered premium ceiling quality. CEREBRO must exceed it in:

- voice naturalness, prosody and emotional intention;
- music and sound-design dynamics;
- subtitle quality and safe-area discipline;
- coherent art direction across scenes;
- controlled visual continuity when a story requires the same subject;
- transitions motivated by the narrative rather than decorative effects;
- stronger cinematography / camera language;
- cleaner typography and closer adherence to Fénix brand system;
- final audio mix with safe true-peak headroom;
- perceptual QA before user review.
