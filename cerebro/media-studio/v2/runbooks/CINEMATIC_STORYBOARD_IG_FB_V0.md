# CEREBRO UMS-001 cinematic story architecture — LAB draft

## Creative brief: cinematic, original, lead-oriented
Set original Fénix narrative and film grammar inspired by ambitious theatrical filmmaking **without copying Marvel/Lucasfilm visual identities, characters, branded weapons, storylines, music or titles**. Strong script, clear human motivations, detailed settings in Córdoba/Andalucía, coherent character bible, rich contrast, believable light and sound, cut rhythm, realistic dialogues, subtitle safe zones, authentic property process, accurate non-guaranteed financial claims, measurable lead CTA.

## First campaign (2 variants; publication still OFF)
Story: **«La puerta que casi se cerró»**. A young couple in Córdoba finds their preferred home. Tension: time is running out and finances are not yet validated. Their dream risks collapsing. Turning point: choose informed mortgage pre-study rather than rushing to pay a deposit. End card: «Antes de reservar, estudia tu operación. Fénix Capital». Do not imply credit is guaranteed.

Scene plan, 25–35 s total:
1. 0–2 s — cold open: a door closes, rising tension / sound impact. Show stakes immediately; no intro bumper.
2. 2–8 s — memorable close-up of keys and faces, gentle cinematic camera movement; FLUX style bible + Pruna Pro quality 5 s (LAB QA).
3. 8–15 s — tension: clock, financial uncertainty. Use photography and controlled local motion/edit, Pruna Pro cost 5 s if motion materially helps.
4. 15–23 s — visual turn; optional Veo Fast 8 s **only if** test quality justifies cost.
5. 23–30 s — real brand assets, voiceover, licensed music/SFX and local programmatic outro 1 second; no video AI credit.

Two outputs based on one master: IG Reel (narrative, first 2s hook, CTA DM) and FB Reel (trust-focused subtitle/CTA to pre-study contact). Independent aspect-safe captions & on-screen CTA. No spurious client testimonials.

## Model choice + fallback
Use a *quality gate*, not a price-only gate:
- FLUX 1.1 Pro/Kontext for keyframes and identity.
- Pruna P-Video 2 Pro at cost 1080p (upscaled from native 768p) for routine scenes, then test quality mode for people/faces.
- Seedance 1.5 Pro 1080p without audio for motion, compare.
- Veo 3.1 Fast without audio for hero scene in a production that justifies it; sound design added locally.
- Runway Gen-4.5 for demanding camera work only after visual QA.
- Kling v3 multishot as WATCH/HOLD until live commercial cost confirmed and schema reviewed.
- If cinematic QA not met, **use real stock/footage and edit**, not endless paid rerolls.

Physical acceptance per selected model: 2 equivalent 5–8s clips with same first-frame and comparable prompts, human review for lighting/realism/temporal consistency, valid copyright, image quality, dialogue and movement, visual continuity, reproducibility and **USD/approved usable second**. No selection may claim blockbuster quality until physical review.
