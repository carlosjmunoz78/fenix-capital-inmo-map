## 4. Production evidence that constrains the likely treatment

Published production reporting on `Iron Man` sound design states that the filmmakers deliberately avoided over-processing suit dialogue and favored intelligibility. The reported approach for processed voices used subtle pitch/resonance changes rather than extreme robotic transformation, with dry recordings preserved and processing adjusted at mix stage.

This is not direct proof of the exact J.A.R.V.I.S. chain, but it supports an important design constraint for CEREBRO: the recognizable result should come primarily from the base vocal timbre + performance, with electronic processing kept subtle.

- very short delay;
- light chorus / micro-doubling;
